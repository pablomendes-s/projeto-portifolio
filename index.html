ConsignaPlus

SaaS CRM for Brazilian payroll-loan brokers (Corbans): manages clients, contracts and loan proposals end-to-end. Built from my own experience running a brokerage, live in production with paying plans.

  • Client & contract management with full proposal history tracking
  • Reports centre and financial module with Chart.js dashboards
  • Multi-user accounts, subscription plans and trial system
  • CPF validation (check-digit algorithm) and data integrity rules
  • Security hardening: CSRF/XSS/HSTS, rate limiting and login lockout (django-axes)
  • Deployed on Render with PostgreSQL, custom domain and transactional email
